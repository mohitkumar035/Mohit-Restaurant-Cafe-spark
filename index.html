Our Heritage & Passion

Crafting Memorable Gatherings & Pure Flavor Since 2018

Mohit Restaurant & Cafe was founded with an unyielding conviction: that dining out is not merely about sustenance, but a sacred pause in the day for celebration, connection, and culinary delight.

Nestled in the heart of the city, our space seamlessly marries the warm rustic charm of a European coffee roastery with the grandeur and rich aromatics of traditional gourmet dining. Every recipe has been tested, refined, and passed through generations of master culinary artistry.

"We pour our soul into every slow-brewed espresso cup and each fragrant earthen handi dish that leaves our kitchen."

— Mohit & Culinary Team
